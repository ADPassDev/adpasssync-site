Privacy Policy

How AD Argus Cybersecurity Corporation handles personal information on adpasssync.com, in the customer portal and in the download service.

In short: we collect what we need to run your account, issue licenses and keep the service secure. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers. The ADPassSync software runs on your own servers and doesn't send us your Active Directory data.

2. What we collect and why

Your portal account

Your email address, to sign you in with emailed links and to contact you about your account, and the name and company you add to your profile. If you register an ADPassSync installation, we keep its Install ID and the license files we issue for it, which include your name or company and your email address.

Signing in and security

When you sign in, we set one cookie to keep you signed in, and we record the IP address and browser details of the session. We record the same details, with the version downloaded, each time you download the software. We use these to secure accounts, prevent abuse and keep a record of downloads.

Purchases

Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record, collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy notice. From Paddle we receive what we need to manage your subscription: Paddle's customer and subscription identifiers, the number of users, billing dates, and whether payments were completed or refunded. We never receive your card details.

Quote requests and the contact form

When you ask for a quote in the portal, we keep the details you enter. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company, approximate number of users, topic and message by email so we can reply; the website itself doesn't keep a copy.

Breached-password blocklist key

Paid accounts are assigned a key for blocklist downloads. We link the key to your account's email address and record when it was last used, at most once an hour.

The ADPassSync software

ADPassSync runs on your own servers and checks its license there. The only requests it makes to us are downloads of the breached-password blocklist, which include a download key if you have entered one. It does not send us your Active Directory data, your users or their passwords.

3. Legal bases

By creating an account, buying a subscription or contacting us, you consent to our using your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw that consent at any time, though we may then be unable to provide the service.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on: performing our contract with you (your account, licenses and subscription); our legitimate interests in securing the service, preventing abuse and answering enquiries; and our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the providers that run parts of the service for us, and only what each needs:

  • Paddle processes payments, taxes, invoices and refunds as merchant of record, and is responsible for that information under its own privacy notice.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website, portal and downloads, and processes IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect them.
  • Resend sends our emails: sign-in links, license notices and replies to enquiries.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the service. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Where it's processed

We are based in British Columbia, Canada. Our providers process information in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union and other countries where they operate, and it may be accessible to authorities there under local law. We rely on our providers' contractual and security commitments to protect it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account, license, session and download records while your account exists. Sign-in links stop working after 15 minutes, and sessions after 24 hours. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete its information, except records we must keep for legal, tax or security reasons. Paddle keeps payment records under its own policy.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, you can also ask us to restrict or stop using it, or to give you a copy in a portable format. Email privacy@adargus.com; we reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the data protection authority where you live.

8. Cookies

We use one cookie, and only after you sign in: it keeps you signed in, and it expires after 24 hours or when you sign out. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. When you open checkout on your portal dashboard, Paddle may set its own cookies, as described in its privacy notice.

9. Security

The site and portal are served only over HTTPS. You sign in with single-use emailed links, so we store no passwords for your account. License files are digitally signed, and access to customer information is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

10. Children

ADPassSync is a product for organizations. The service isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information about anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We post every update to this policy on this page with a new date, and we email account holders about significant changes.
