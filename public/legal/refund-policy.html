Refund Policy

A 30-day money-back guarantee on every ADPassSync Professional payment.

This policy is part of our Terms of Sale.

Our 30-day guarantee

If ADPassSync Professional isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of a payment and you'll get that payment back in full. This covers your first purchase and every yearly renewal. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask

Email us at sales@adargus.com from the address you bought with, or include the order number from your receipt. You can also contact Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record, through the link in your receipt.

Paddle issues the refund to the payment method you used. How long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

What a refund does

A refund ends your subscription. Your paid license ends at once, your installation returns to the Free Edition limit of 50 users, and your key for the full breached-password database stops working. You can keep using ADPassSync for free within that limit, and you can subscribe again at any time.

If you only want fewer users, lower the number on your portal dashboard instead: Paddle credits the unused part against future payments, and your subscription carries on.

After 30 days

Payments more than 30 days old aren't refundable. You can still cancel at any time to stop the next renewal, and your license runs until the end of the term you've paid for.

Your rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under consumer-protection law where you live. Paddle may also issue refunds under its own Buyer Terms.
